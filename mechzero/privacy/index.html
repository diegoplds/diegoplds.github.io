Privacy Policy - Mech Zero: Humanity Error

Controller: DPLDS · Effective November 21, 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Mech Zero: Humanity Error for Android ("the game") collects, how it is used and who it is shared with.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves on your device your preferences (language, audio, graphics quality, frame rate), your best run and internal counters used to control how often ads are shown. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts, precise location or any other personal data, and it has no account system of its own and no in-app purchases.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and cap ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect and process:

  • the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and system information (model, OS version, language, carrier);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (impressions and taps) and SDK diagnostic data.

Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads appear at set moments (for example, when leaving a run, after staying on the pause screen for a while, or when you choose to watch an ad to revive or reroll cards). Rewarded ads are always optional.

3. Consent

Where required by law (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form before loading ads. You may decline personalization, in which case non-personalized or limited ads may be shown. When available, you can review this choice in the game settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads).

4. Game engine diagnostics (Unity)

The game is built with the Unity engine, which may send crash reports and technical device data (model, OS, performance) to Unity Technologies to diagnose errors. This data does not directly identify you. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

5. Who data is shared with

DPLDS does not sell personal data and does not receive or store the data described above on its own servers. The data is processed directly by the providers listed (Google and Unity Technologies) for the purposes described, and may be transferred outside your country under their policies.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the game, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your rights

Under the Brazilian LGPD, the GDPR and similar laws, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data and information about its processing. Because advertising and diagnostic data are processed by Google and Unity, many of these requests are handled directly through their tools; you can still contact us below and we will help.

8. Security and retention

Local data stays only on your device. Retention of data processed by third parties follows each provider's policy.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated (for example, if the game adds sign-in, achievements or leaderboards). The current version will always be on this page, with its effective date at the top.
